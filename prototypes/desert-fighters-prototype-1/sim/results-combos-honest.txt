Same cards and seed as results-combos-pass2.txt. What changed is the COUNTER, not the game:
  old "strong combo" = lift ≥ 3×SE, where lift = triple win − base − Σ solo ΔWin. No absolute bar, so a triple that
  merely carries a weak card looks like synergy (the weak card's low solo makes "okay" look like a surprise). Prototype 3
  showed the count RISES when abilities are deleted, so the old number partly measured how bad the parts are.
  new "winning lineup" = triple win ≥ base + 10 (absolute: cannot be gamed by weak parts)
  new "good combo"     = winning AND lift ≥ 1×SE (wins, and wins more than "three good cards" predicts)
  coverage             = good combos a card owns (fielded in ≥ 60% of its wins); the minimum is the "weakest card can create a win" test
  --strip id1,id2      = control run: those cards lose their ability. A change is only real if it beats the blank-card control.

Reading: the old counter rewards removing abilities, the honest counter does not. The game-wide good-combo total is a crowded,
relative number (other triples fill the gap when four cards go blank), so it is a weak discriminator between designs. The
sensitive numbers are per-card coverage and the minimum: a card's ability either puts it in winning lineups or it does not.
Rule for future passes: judge a change by (1) the per-card coverage of the changed cards vs the blank-card control, (2) the
minimum coverage, (3) the 3way column for triples that are more than stacked pairs. Ignore the old strong count.
